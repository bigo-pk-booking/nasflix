LEGAL SOURCES

For Archive.org, open a specific title, copy its archive.org/details/... page URL and paste it into the Media URL field above. From Archive Watch, use the title's “View on archive.org” link first. NASFLIX can play supported Archive.org title pages, YouTube links, Google Drive previews and direct media URLs. Pexels and Pixabay clips generally need to be downloaded (following the asset license) and uploaded to NASFLIX; their page URLs are not playable media links.
