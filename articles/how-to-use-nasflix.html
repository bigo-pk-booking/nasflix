How to Explore the NASFLIX Entertainment Library

A practical walkthrough for finding something to watch or listen to

NASFLIX brings several kinds of entertainment into one browsing experience: a catalogue of videos, a music library, internet radio discovery and a directory of live-TV sources. Each section works a little differently, so this guide explains where to start and what to expect.

Start with the section that matches what you want

  • TV: Browse the channel directory. A channel may open in an embedded player or on the broadcaster's own website, depending on its source.
  • Movies & Videos: Explore catalogue entries and use the available category filters to narrow the list. A title's description and source determine what playback options are available.
  • Music: Browse tracks in the music library and use playlists to organize the music available on NASFLIX.
  • Radio: Search the station directory by country or genre. Radio streams are supplied by broadcasters and directory providers, so availability can vary.

Use search and categories together

If you know a title, artist or keyword, start with search. If you are exploring, use a category such as animation, comedy, documentary or music to reduce the number of results. Try a shorter search phrase if the exact title does not appear; catalogue descriptions and naming conventions are not always identical.

Understand what a catalogue entry tells you

A useful entry should identify the title, its type and relevant details. Read the description before opening a source. A catalogue listing is a discovery aid; it does not mean NASFLIX owns the work or that every third-party player will be available in every location.

Make playlists useful

When signed in, you can create playlists for the music available in the library. Give each list a clear purpose—such as relaxed listening, a commute or favourite tracks—and remove songs you no longer want in it. Playlists organize your listening; they do not change the rights or availability of the underlying recordings.

When a stream does not play

  1. Check that your internet connection is working.
  2. Try the title again or refresh the page once.
  3. If the entry uses an external source, open the provider's page and check whether the stream is available there.
  4. Try another entry if the provider is offline, blocks embedding or restricts playback in your region.

NASFLIX cannot repair a broadcaster's server or remove restrictions imposed by an external provider.

Browse thoughtfully

Use the video catalogue, explore the music library or open NASFLIX Radio. For information about the site's purpose and third-party sources, read About NASFLIX.
