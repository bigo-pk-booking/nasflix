About NASFLIX

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Our purpose

NASFLIX is an entertainment discovery website bringing together a browsable catalogue of videos, a music library and a live radio directory in one simple experience.

We aim to make it easier for visitors to discover entertainment, explore music and find radio stations from the Philippines and around the world.

What you can explore

  • Videos: Browse the video catalogue and available programme information.
  • Music: Explore the music library and organize playlists when signed in.
  • NASFLIX Radio: Discover live radio stations through a third-party station directory.

Content and third-party services

Some catalogue entries, streams, embeds or links may be provided by third parties. Their availability, terms and content are controlled by those providers. NASFLIX does not claim ownership of third-party material. We are continuing to improve our content information and review rights and permissions for material presented on the site.
