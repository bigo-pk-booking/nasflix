DMCA & Copyright Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Respect for copyright

NASFLIX respects intellectual property rights and expects visitors and content contributors to do the same. If you believe material accessible through NASFLIX infringes your copyright, you may send us a notice using the contact details below.

What to include in a notice

  • Your name and a working email address.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The exact NASFLIX page URL and a description of the material at issue.
  • A statement explaining your good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, where applicable, that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.

Send your request

Email your notice to nasflix1995@gmail.com with the subject line Copyright / DMCA Notice. We may request additional information to assess the report and may remove or disable access to material where appropriate.

Counter-notices and repeat issues

If content was removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe this was a mistake, contact us with the relevant page, an explanation and supporting information. We may take appropriate action regarding repeat infringement reports or violations of our Terms of Service.

This page provides a contact process and is not legal advice. Applicable legal requirements may vary by jurisdiction.
